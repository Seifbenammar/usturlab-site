The company

Usturlab OÜ

An usturlab is an astrolabe: the instrument that told a traveller where they stood, long before anyone could tell them. The name suits what we build.

Who we are

Usturlab OÜ is a private limited company incorporated in Estonia and operating across the European Union. Its activity is the publication of business software, and the advisory work that surrounds its deployment. The portfolio is deliberately open: one platform is in service, others are in preparation, and part of the work is developed to order.

The company is run by a lead auditor certified for quality, environmental, occupational health and safety and energy management systems, who carries out certification audits for accredited certification bodies. That practice is what the products are built from.
