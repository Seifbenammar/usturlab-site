What we do

A workshop, not a catalogue

Regulatory requirements and management-system evidence are usually scattered across spreadsheets, shared drives and individual memory. We build applications that gather them in one place, attached to the site and the process they actually apply to. One platform is in service today. Others are in preparation, and part of what we do is built to order.

In service

TrackSyst

A modular platform for running certified management systems: information security, quality, environment, occupational health and safety, energy. One core, one module per standard, shared context and shared evidence.

Inside the platform

Regulatory compliance

A register of applicable requirements maintained site by site, changes in the underlying legal texts followed, and compliance evaluation turned into a documented, auditable exercise.

Why it is built this way

Written by people who sit on the other side of the table

Our software is designed by certified management-system auditors. Every screen answers a question an auditor will actually ask: which requirement applies to this site, who evaluated it, on what evidence, and when was that last reviewed. That constraint shapes the data model, not the marketing.

  • Requirements attached to sites and processes, not to a global list nobody owns
  • Traceable evaluation history, exportable for audit
  • Source legal texts linked, so a finding can always be taken back to its origin
  • European hosting, no third-party trackers, no resale of customer data
