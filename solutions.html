Platforms

One platform is in service today, delivered as software as a service and sold to organisations rather than to individuals. It is where we started, not the limit of what we build.

TrackSyst

TrackSyst is a modular platform for organisations running one or several certified management systems. The core holds the context that all standards share, and each module adds what its own standard requires.

  • Information security management, aligned with ISO/IEC 27001
  • Quality management, aligned with ISO 9001
  • Environment, occupational health and safety, and energy modules
  • Regulatory compliance: applicable-requirement register, evaluation and corrective actions
  • Strategic deployment tools: context analysis, interested-party mapping, process mapping, objective deployment matrices
  • A performance dashboard designed so that the management review can be built from it rather than rewritten every year

tracksyst.cloud

The regulatory compliance module

It answers the question every certified site has to settle at least once a year: which legal requirements apply to us, and are we meeting them.

The module maintains a structured register of applicable requirements drawn from labour and environmental legislation, classified-installation rules, chemical classification, dangerous-goods transport and fluorinated-gas regulations, among others. Each requirement carries a verification question, an applicability rule and an evaluation history.

  • Applicability decided site by site, so a group with ten plants does not share one undifferentiated list
  • Corrective actions tied directly to the requirement that triggered them
  • A link back to the consolidated source text for every requirement
  • Spreadsheet and PDF exports for the audit file and the management review
  • Requirements withdrawn automatically when the underlying text is repealed
